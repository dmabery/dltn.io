Its hard to write book notes about audiobooks I listen to because, well, I can’t take notes on a book during my commute. Some books I listen to but stop because there are so many little nuggets that I want to record that I think it’s better I just read the book itself.

So, instead of writing book notes of audiobooks, I’ll share little snippets about why I liked the book. This is the first post in the series.

[Codename Nemo](https://open.spotify.com/show/6EwtfSIX2TdHi40PE7Lfyk?si=ce46ca49602b431b) is about the American Navy’s capture of U-505, the first time since 1817 when a ship was captured on the open seas. It’s a remarkable story with a great narrator. It started off kind of slow, but mainly because I couldn’t tell if it was a narrative non-fiction or historical fiction. Before I determined that it was narrative non-fiction, and everything was true, I listened to the background of the characters kind of half-heartedly. I was really just waiting for the “meat” of the U-boat capture story.

But once it got there, boy was I excited to start my commute every day.

The book’s organization is similar to _Dead Wake_ by Erik Larson. Every chapter goes back and forth between documenting the history and current events of the [capitan and crew of the U-boat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_submarine_U-505) and the captain and crew of the _USS Guadalcanal_ until the two ships meet on the high seas.

The boarding party from the _Guadalcanal_ rowed from the aircraft carrier to the U-boat, went down the conning tower fully expecting to be bombarded with bullets and explosions from German sailors waiting for them. Luckily, there weren’t any. They then proceeded to prevent the scuttling of the boat, something the Germans tried to do to sink the boat so what was happening to U-505 wouldn’t happen. Somehow, they were able to attach a tow rope to the submarine and tow it alongside of the aircraft carrier. Meanwhile, they captured the crew of the U-boat, who were all dismayed later the next day when they realized their captors successfully captured their precious _unterseeboot_.

Highly recommend [giving it a listen](https://open.spotify.com/show/6EwtfSIX2TdHi40PE7Lfyk?si=ce46ca49602b431b).
